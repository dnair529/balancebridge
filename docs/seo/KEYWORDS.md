## 3. Local Modifiers Matrix (Service × City)

Pattern: `[service] + [city]`. City pages target the bundled term; service pages capture statewide terms; blog/FAQ content mops up long-tails. Do **not** spin up 50 thin service-city pages — start with 5 strong city pages and add service-city pages only where the city page demonstrably can't rank for the combination.

## 6. Notes & Guardrails

- **Tax positioning:** Balance Bridge coordinates with CPAs; it does not file taxes. Never target "tax filing service" or "tax preparation service texas" as money terms — target *tax-ready bookkeeping* and *CPA coordination* instead.
- **Time-sensitive claims:** Franchise tax thresholds and filing requirements changed in 2024; sales-tax local rates vary. All content phrases these as "verify current figures with the Texas Comptroller."
- **Local SEO:** Organic pages alone won't win "near me" queries — Google Business Profile(s), consistent NAP, and reviews carry the map pack. Track separately.
