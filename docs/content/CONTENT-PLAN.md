## E-E-A-T Requirements (every post)

- **Author byline** with a real team member, linked to an author page listing credentials (QuickBooks ProAdvisor certifications, years in practice, industries served).
- **"Reviewed by"** line on any post touching tax, payroll compliance, or Texas regulation — reviewed by a named senior team member or partner CPA. Balance Bridge does not file taxes; tax posts must state "we coordinate with your CPA" and never give filing advice.
- **Primary sources cited:** Texas Comptroller, IRS, TWC (Texas Workforce Commission), SOS for formation topics. Link out; don't paraphrase thresholds as permanent facts.
- **Freshness discipline:** any figure that can change (franchise tax thresholds, sales tax local rates, wage bases, deadlines) is phrased as "verify current figures with [source]" plus a visible "Last reviewed" date. Re-review Texas-cluster posts every January.
- **First-hand experience:** include at least one anonymized client scenario or "what we see in real books" observation per post.

**Internal-link convention:** every post links UP to at least one service page, ACROSS to 1–2 sibling posts, and BOFU posts link to /pricing/ and /contact/.
